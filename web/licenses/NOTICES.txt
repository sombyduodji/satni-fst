Language data (all versions)
----------------------------
Dictionaries: GiellaLT dict-sme-fin, dict-sme-nob, dict-sme-eng,
dict-sme-smn, dict-sme-smj, dict-sme-sma, dict-fin-sme, dict-nob-sme,
dict-smn-sme, dict-smj-sme, dict-sma-sme, dict-smn-fin, dict-fin-smn,
dict-sme-spa and dict-spa-sme (Spanish), and the monolingual North Sámi
dictionary dict-sme (North Sámi definitions)
  © GiellaLT / Divvun / Giellatekno, UiT The Arctic University of Norway,
  and contributors. The North Sámi–Spanish dictionaries are written by
  Ángel Díaz de Rada (UNED) and Kjell Kemi.
  As the dictionaries' own attribution texts specify, they are also the
  work of Nils Jernsletten (North Sámi–Norwegian and Norwegian–North Sámi,
  and part of North Sámi–Finnish), Kotus – Institute for the Languages of
  Finland (Finnish–North Sámi and North Sámi–Finnish), Marja-Liisa Olthuis
  and Taarna Valtonen (Finnish–Inari Sámi and Inari Sámi–Finnish), and of
  members of the language communities.
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
  (CC-BY-4.0.txt, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
  Source: https://github.com/giellalt
  Changes: the dictionaries were merged into one database and extended
  with words recognised by the North Sámi FST, inflection tables,
  consonant gradation and hand-made corrections (korjaukset.tsv in the
  source code); Norwegian letters in Finnish translations were fixed.
  Missing Inari, Lule and Southern Sámi translations are filled from the
  Inari, Lule and Southern Sámi to North Sámi dictionaries. Experimental
  Finnish suggestions are made through Inari Sámi (dict-smn-sme,
  dict-smn-fin, dict-fin-smn) or through Norwegian, marked as unchecked.
  The Spanish dictionaries are read from their Excel sources; synonym rows
  are merged and grammar notes are kept apart from the translations.
  From dict-sme, the definitions, synonyms and examples are shown; examples
  already shown with a translation are left out.

Skolt Sámi (experimental): GiellaLT dict-sms-mul, dict-fin-sms and
dict-nob-sms
  © GiellaLT / Divvun / Giellatekno, UiT The Arctic University of Norway,
  and contributors; dict-fin-sms and dict-nob-sms are also the work of
  AKU and the Kone Foundation and members of the language communities.
  Licence: CC BY 4.0 (CC-BY-4.0.txt); dict-fin-sms: Creative Commons
  Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC-BY-3.0.txt,
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
  Source: https://github.com/giellalt
  Changes: for each Finnish and Norwegian word, at most three Skolt Sámi
  words are kept (same word class, main meaning, both sources agreeing).
  For a North Sámi word they are found through its Finnish translation.
  Word order uses counts of dictionary words in the texts marked free in
  GiellaLT corpus-sms (https://github.com/giellalt/corpus-sms); no corpus
  text is included.

Norwegian translations (experimental): Apertium apertium-sme-nob
  © 2010-2019 Francis M. Tyers, Kevin Brubeck Unhammer, Trond Trosterud
  and the Apertium contributors; most of the bilingual dictionary comes
  from Giellatekno (UiT).
  Licence: GPL-2.0-or-later, used here under GPL-3.0 (GPL-3.0.txt).
  https://github.com/apertium/apertium-sme-nob
  Changes: only the bilingual dictionary is used, for nouns, verbs,
  adjectives and adverbs with no Norwegian translation in GiellaLT.

North Sámi morphology: GiellaLT lang-sme
  Authors: Linda Wiechetek, Katri Hiovain-Asikainen, Inga Lill Sigga
  Mikkelsen, Sjur N. Moshagen, Flammie A Pirinen, Trond Trosterud,
  Børre Gaup and the lang-sme contributors.
  Licence: GNU GPL version 3 (GPL-3.0.txt)
  Source: https://github.com/giellalt/lang-sme
  The inflection tables were generated with it.

Inari Sámi inflection (photos, experimental): GiellaLT lang-smn
(Giellatekno, UiT)
  Licence: GNU GPL version 3 (GPL-3.0.txt)
  Source: https://github.com/giellalt/lang-smn
  Inari Sámi word forms for photo recognition were generated with it;
  Inari Sámi translations come from dict-smn-fin, dict-fin-smn and
  dict-smn-sme (above). Norwegian is reached through North Sámi and is
  approximate.

Skolt Sámi inflection (photos, experimental): GiellaLT lang-sms
(Giellatekno, UiT)
  Licence: GNU GPL version 3 (GPL-3.0.txt)
  Source: https://github.com/giellalt/lang-sms
  Skolt Sámi word forms for photo recognition were generated with it;
  translations come from dict-sms-mul, dict-fin-sms and dict-nob-sms
  (above). North Sámi mostly goes through Finnish and is approximate.

Finnish and Norwegian word pairs (photo sentence view, experimental):
Leipzig Corpora Collection, Finnish corpora (news 2020 and 2022, Wikipedia
2021) and Norwegian corpora (bokmål newscrawl 2019, Wikipedia 2021),
Wortschatz Leipzig, Universität Leipzig
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY), see
  https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de/en/usage (CC-BY-4.0.txt)
  Source: https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de/en/download/fin and /nob
  Only counts are included: word pairs that occur at least three times
  and how often single words occur (at least five times); no sentences. Goldhahn, Eckart & Quasthoff (2012), LREC 2012.

Finnish inflection: GiellaLT lang-fin, based on the Omorfi lexicon
(Tommi A. Pirinen, Inari Listenmaa, Ryan Johnson, Francis M. Tyers and
others)
  Licence: GNU GPL version 3 (GPL-3.0.txt)
  Source: https://github.com/giellalt/lang-fin

English translations from Wiktionary (where GiellaLT has no English)
  © Wiktionary contributors, https://en.wiktionary.org
  Machine-readable extract from kaikki.org (wiktextract, Tatu Ylönen)
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
  (CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/),
  The adapted data is licensed under GPL-3.0, which CC BY-SA 4.0 allows
  as a compatible licence.
  Changes: only words without English get one: Wiktionary's own North
  Sámi entries and English entries that list a Northern Sámi
  translation. Where the Finnish and the Norwegian translation give the
  same English in Wiktionary, it is shown as an unchecked suggestion.
  Notes in brackets and "synonym of"-type explanations are left out.

English machine translations (experimental, off by default; eng_mt.py, eng_mt.tsv)
  For words with no English in the dictionaries, shown only when at least
  two of three routes give the same English word, and marked as machine
  translations. Made with:
  - smi-mt: TartuNLP Smugri model nllb1.3-smugri4-v0.01 (CC BY 4.0,
    https://huggingface.co/tartuNLP/nllb1.3-smugri4-v0.01); base model
    Meta NLLB-200 (CC BY-NC 4.0). Used in a free, non-commercial app.
  - Helsinki-NLP opus-mt-fi-en and opus-mt-gmq-en (Apache-2.0.txt),
    https://huggingface.co/Helsinki-NLP
  - FinnWordNet 2.0, © University of Helsinki (CC-BY-3.0.txt), with the
    English words of Princeton WordNet 3.0 (WordNet licence, © Princeton
    University, https://wordnet.princeton.edu/license-and-commercial-use)
  - Wiktionary via kaikki.org (CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt, as above)
  - Apertium apertium-fin-eng (GPL-3.0.txt),
    https://github.com/apertium/apertium-fin-eng
  Only the resulting English words are included, not the models or the
  dictionaries.

Finnish machine-translated suggestions (experimental, off by default; fin_mt.py, fin_mt.tsv)
  For words with no Finnish, shown only when at least two independent
  routes give the same Finnish word (Voikko-checked). Made with smi-mt
  (as above), Helsinki-NLP opus-mt-no-fi and opus-mt-en-fi (Apache-2.0.txt),
  Wiktionary (CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt), FinnWordNet (CC-BY-3.0.txt) and GiellaLT
  dict-nob-fin, dict-fin-nob (Trond Trosterud and Kirsti Kuosa, UiT),
  dict-smn-fin and dict-sma-fin (CC-BY-4.0.txt),
  https://github.com/giellalt. Only the resulting Finnish words are included.

Spanish machine translations (experimental, off by default; spa_mt.py, spa_mt.tsv)
  For words with no Spanish in dict-sme-spa, shown only when at least three
  of six sources give the same Spanish word. Made with Helsinki-NLP
  opus-mt-fi-es, opus-mt-no-es and opus-mt-en-es (Apache-2.0.txt), smi-mt
  (as above), Wiktionary and WikDict (CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt) and the Spanish
  wordnet of the Multilingual Central Repository 3.0 (CC-BY-3.0.txt,
  http://adimen.si.ehu.es/web/MCR/). Only the resulting Spanish words are
  included.

Corpus frequencies: SIKOR North Saami lemma frequency
list 2015-10-10
  © UiT The Arctic University of Norway, Giellatekno and Divvun
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC-BY-3.0.txt)
  Source: http://hdl.handle.net/11509/106 (CLARINO Bergen)
  Changes: used only for words without a frequency; letters and
  abbreviations are left out.

Related words in other Sámi languages: Álgu, the
Etymological Database of the Saami Languages, XML version 16.3.2023
  © Kotimaisten kielten keskus (Institute for the Languages of Finland),
  https://kaino.kotus.fi/algu
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC-BY-4.0.txt)
  Changes: only the links between related words are used, and a related
  word is shown only when it is in the current Inari, Skolt, Lule or
  South Sámi dictionary. Álgu's Finnish and German meanings are not used.

Sámi place names in Norway: Stedsnavn
  © Kartverket, https://kartkatalog.geonorge.no/metadata/30caed2f-454e-44be-b5cc-26bb5c0110ca
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC-BY-4.0.txt)
  Changes: only places with both a North Sámi and a Norwegian name
  (main and side names, not historical) are kept, with Lule and South
  Sámi names where they exist.

Norwegian inflection: Norsk ordbank – bokmål 2005 (dump of 2022-02-01)
  © Språkbanken / Nasjonalbiblioteket
  Licence: CC BY 4.0 (CC-BY-4.0.txt)
  Source: https://www.nb.no/sprakbanken/ressurskatalog/oai-nb-no-sbr-5/
  Changes: only the forms of words used as translations are kept;
  compounds missing from the word bank are inflected by their last part.

Consonant gradation groups: Oahpa (Giellatekno, UiT)
  https://oahpa.no/sme/gramm/tabell_stadieveksling.html

Pronunciation: speech synthesis of Divvun and Giellatekno (UiT The Arctic
University of Norway), voices Biret, Máhtte and Sunná
  Used through the network service api-giellalt.uit.no with Divvun's
  permission. The voice samples in voices/ were generated with the same
  service and are included with Divvun's permission.

Spoken word lists (audio/: every list in Word lists): Finnish word followed by
the North Sámi word; the Norwegian interface plays the North Sámi words only.
  Finnish: synthesised offline with Chatterbox Finnish (Finnish-NLP, MIT
  licence; fine-tuned from Resemble AI's Chatterbox, MIT licence). The model's
  training data and its attribution are listed in the model card (Common
  Voice CC0, Filmot/YouTube CC BY, Finnish Parliament recordings under the
  CLARIN PUB +BY licence). The voice is modelled on a LibriVox recording read
  by Harri Tapani Ylilammi (Olli Wuorinen: Salmelan heinätalkoot, public
  domain). The generated audio carries Resemble AI's inaudible Perth
  watermark.
  https://huggingface.co/Finnish-NLP/Chatterbox-Finnish
  https://archive.org/details/salmelan_heinatalkoot_1509_librivox
  North Sámi: Divvun's and Giellatekno's speech synthesis, voices Biret and
  Máhtte (the greetings conversation), with Divvun's permission (see
  Pronunciation above).

Mini game (data/minipeli.json): the everyday word list was chosen from the
dictionary by ranking words that are common both in the North Sámi corpus
and in Finnish film and TV subtitles. The ranking used FrequencyWords
(Hermit Dave, OpenSubtitles 2018 frequency lists, content CC BY-SA 4.0);
the lists themselves are not included.
  https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
  The animal words are the same as in the Máhtte learning game. The spoken
  feedback for weekdays, seasons and months is Divvun's and Giellatekno's
  speech synthesis, voice Biret, with Divvun's permission.

Logo: the Sámi flag (designed by Astrid Båhl, 1986) drawn as a drop.
